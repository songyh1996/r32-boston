How this works — methodology & assumptions

This is a Monte Carlo simulation. Each "run" plays out the entire 48-team group stage thousands of times, then resolves the Round-of-32 bracket. Match 74 (Boston) is one game between two teams — Seat A (the Group E winner) vs Seat B (a wildcard 3rd-place team) — so we track each seat's race separately, plus how often each exact A-vs-B tie occurs.

The Boston match (real bracket)

  • Match 74 at Gillette Stadium = Winner Group E vs Best 3rd place from A/B/C/D/F.
  • 32 teams reach the Round of 32: 12 group winners + 12 runners-up + the 8 best third-placed teams.
  • Third-placed teams are slotted to winners by FIFA's official combination table. Boston's winner-of-E slot can only meet a third-placed side from Groups A, B, C, D or F.

Match model

  • Each team has a strength rating (default = approximate FIFA World Ranking points, June 2026). All ratings are editable above.
  • For each game, the rating gap sets an expected goal supremacy; each side's goals are drawn from a Poisson distribution, so scorelines (and goal difference) are realistic.
  • Group ranking uses points → goal difference → goals scored (head-to-head/fair-play tiebreakers simplified). The 8 best thirds are ranked the same way.
  • Qualifying thirds are matched to winner slots by a valid assignment respecting the official eligibility sets; when several assignments are valid the choice is randomised, so no single group is artificially favoured.

Real results (live)

  • Completed group-stage matches are played to their actual scorelines; only the remaining fixtures are simulated. Results come from openfootball/worldcup.json and are listed in the “Real results baked in” panel.
  • “↻ Refresh from live data” pulls the latest scores in your browser (matches dated on/before today). Untick “Lock in real results” to see the pre-tournament forecast instead.
  • Ratings stay at their pre-tournament value for the matches still to be played — a real 7–1 already reshapes goal difference and the third-place race without us second-guessing each team's form.

Honest caveats

  • It's a model for fun and insight, not a betting tool. Ratings are approximate and the exact 495-row FIFA third-place table is approximated by a valid randomised matching.
  • You can still nudge any rating (e.g. after watching a team play) and re-run to see the picture shift.
